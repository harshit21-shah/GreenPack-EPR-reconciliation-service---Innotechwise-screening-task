# Mock EPR Policy Note: Plastic Category Reporting

## Plastic Categories
Rigid plastic, flexible plastic, and multilayer plastic should be reported separately because each category can have different recycling, processing, and audit requirements. A declaration that merges categories can make reconciliation and evidence review difficult.

## Variance Review
When declared quantities differ from procurement or sales records, the compliance team should review invoices, goods receipt notes, and month-end cut-off entries. Material gaps should be corrected before the final filing is submitted.

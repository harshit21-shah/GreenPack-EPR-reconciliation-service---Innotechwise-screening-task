# Mock EPR Policy Note: Target Compliance

## EPR Targets
EPR targets represent the quantity of plastic waste a producer is expected to process or offset for a reporting period. The target should be tracked against category-wise obligations and supporting certificates.

## Missed Targets
If a producer expects to miss an EPR target, the compliance officer should escalate the gap internally, arrange additional processing or credit purchase where permitted, and document the reason for the shortfall.

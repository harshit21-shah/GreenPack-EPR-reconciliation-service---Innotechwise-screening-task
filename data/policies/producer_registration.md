# Mock EPR Policy Note: Producer Registration

## Registration Requirement
Producers, importers, and brand owners that place plastic packaging on the market should maintain an EPR registration before filing periodic plastic waste compliance returns. The registration record should identify the legal entity, product categories, and plastic packaging categories handled by the business.

## Monthly Records
Monthly records should preserve the link between internal procurement, packaging put into market, and declarations submitted to the compliance portal. Records should be retained with invoice references, category breakups, and the reporting month.

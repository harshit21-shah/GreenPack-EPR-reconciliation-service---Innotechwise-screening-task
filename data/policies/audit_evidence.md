# Mock EPR Policy Note: Audit Evidence

## Evidence Pack
An audit evidence pack should include the submitted declaration, ERP extracts, procurement invoices, recycling or processing certificates, and approvals from the compliance owner. Evidence should be traceable to the producer, month, and plastic category.

## Unsupported Questions
Questions about legal penalties, fees, or official filing deadlines should be answered only when the relevant source document is present in the corpus. If the corpus does not contain the answer, the service should say that it does not know based on the provided documents.
